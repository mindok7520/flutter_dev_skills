2. Inspect version, quality, policy, signing, and recovery evidence.
3. Create the authorized candidate and verify actual artifacts; deploy only with explicit applicable authorization.
4. These templates produce candidate builds and artifacts. Missing providers or signing credentials must not be reported as a completed deployment.
